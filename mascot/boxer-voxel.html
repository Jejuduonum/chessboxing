Chess boxerin voxels

The boxer pawn rebuilt from cubes as a real 3D block model. Each cube is roughly 1/16 of the pawn’s height. The face is painted onto the front of the head, the way voxel games do it.

Hero

The build

Chunky ivory body on a two-step base, red gloves with white cuffs held up in guard, and a red headband whose tails stick out behind the head.

Materials

Lighting

One light from the top-left: tops brightest, front faces mid-tone, side faces darkest. Every colour keeps that three-step rule, so later pieces will match.

Block size

17 cubes tall. Smaller cubes give a smoother, more detailed character; bigger ones look more retro and read better at tiny sizes.

Moods

Only the painted face pixels change, so moods can swap instantly in the app.

Turnaround

The same model rotated 90° at a time — the headband tails and glove cuffs show how it holds up as a real 3D character.
